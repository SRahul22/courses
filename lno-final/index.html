E1 251 Final Exam Pack

End-term: 27 Nov 2026. Built from all lecture transcripts (Ch 1–10), all 9 past papers, the problem sets and the notes. Topics are grouped by idea, not by chapter, and ordered by marks per hour: if you run out of time, what you skipped is worth the least.

How to use this pack

  1. Open the modules in order. In each one: read 1. Must remember → read 2. Concepts (plain words first, then the exact statement, then a worked example) → glance at 3. What he said.
  2. 4. Practice is where marks come from. Try every question on paper before opening the answer. For MCQs, click an option: it turns green or red and the explanation opens. If you got it wrong, read why, then move on (don't re-read the whole concept).
  3. Finish with 5. Self-test without looking up. Anything you miss there goes onto your handwritten sheet.
  4. Tick the box when a module is done (ticks are saved in this browser).
Rules he enforces in every answer
  • Rewrite constraints as $g(x)\le0$, $h(x)=0$ first. Lagrangian $L=f+\lambda^Th+\mu^Tg$, $\mu\ge0$.
  • Always write all five KKT parts. Use only the course's theorem forms (other books' forms "not accepted").
  • Never write "concave optimization problem" or "concave set".
  • No full proofs are asked, but the steps of proofs are (as MCQ chains or short answers).

In the exam hall

  • Choose questions: if it's "answer k of n" (like Midterm 2), take the ADMM derivation and the KKT ladder if offered (most practised, most predictable), then the MCQ groups you're surest of.
  • MCQs: read "valid in general under the stated assumptions". Look for the missing or extra assumption, a flipped sign, necessary vs sufficient. If negative marking (−¼) applies, skip a question when you can't eliminate at least two options.
  • Descriptive: write the setup lines even if you get stuck (constraint form, Lagrangian, KKT parts, $L_A$): they earn marks.
